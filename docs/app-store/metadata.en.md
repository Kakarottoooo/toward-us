## Description
Pinky is a private relationship coach that becomes a shared assistant when you and your partner connect.

JUST ME — YOUR PRIVATE SPACE
Say what's on your mind, by voice or text. Pinky listens first, helps you see how your partner might feel, and helps you find the words, without taking sides or writing anything cruel. Everything here is yours alone: your partner never sees it, and Pinky never hints that you talked.

US — THE TWO OF YOU AND PINKY
Invite your partner with a code. In your shared chat Pinky helps you plan a weekend, settle a decision, or turn a hard conversation into something you both agree on. An agreement only counts when both of you approve the same version.

KEEP WHAT YOU PROMISE
• Reminders for what you agreed, and a gentle check-in later
• Anniversaries, birthdays and the countdown to your next time together
• A daily question for the two of you
• Moments: photos and stories of your time together

GETTING TO KNOW SOMEONE?
Pinky also coaches you while you're getting to know people: each person is a private card only you can see.

PRIVACY YOU CAN CHECK
• Private stays private: only a summary you confirm can enter the shared space
• See exactly what Pinky can use, download your data, or delete it at any time
• Leave a relationship or delete your account without anyone's approval
• Pinky uses third-party AI (OpenAI, Anthropic) to reply and turn voice into text, only after you agree

Pinky's suggestions are generated by AI and can be wrong. Pinky is not therapy or medical advice. For people 18 and older.
